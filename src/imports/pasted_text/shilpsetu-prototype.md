Create a complete, polished, mobile-first prototype called “ShilpSetu” for marginalized artisans, handloom workers, and small rural craft producers. This is not simply a digital marketplace. The main purpose is to make digital selling easy for artisans who may have low digital literacy, limited typing ability, limited English knowledge, or little experience using apps.

Core idea: “Take a photo. Speak. Sell.”
Tagline: “Your craft. Your market. Your digital assistant.”

DESIGN DIRECTION
Use a warm, trustworthy, human-centered design inspired by Indian handicrafts. Use earthy craft colors, natural textures, subtle woven patterns, rounded cards, large icons, high contrast, clear spacing, and very large touch targets. Keep text short and simple. Avoid generic e-commerce designs, complicated dashboards, excessive cards, gradients, glassmorphism, neon colors, tiny text, technical terms, and long paragraphs. The app should feel friendly, calm, and easy enough for a first-time smartphone user. Design for a 390px mobile screen, but make it responsive.

IMPORTANT NAVIGATION REQUIREMENT
Every button, language option, microphone command, card, and action must lead to a real connected screen or perform a visible action. Do not create static mockups where buttons do nothing. The complete prototype must support a smooth end-to-end demo.

OPENING LANGUAGE SCREEN
Create a beautiful welcome screen with the ShilpSetu logo and a simple message such as “Sell your craft with your voice.” Show language choices in their actual native scripts:
മലയാളം, हिंदी, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, मराठी, English.
When the user taps a language, immediately change the entire app interface into that language, including buttons, navigation labels, instructions, voice assistant text, and screen headings. Do not show instructional text such as “click to translate.”

Add a large, attractive microphone button labelled “Speak to use the app.” Tapping the microphone must open a voice assistant panel. The user should be able to say:
“Open camera”
“Add product”
“Show my sales”
“Find materials”
“Create catalog”
“Show government schemes”
“Explain this page”
“Go back”
“Publish product”

These commands must trigger actual navigation. For example:
Language Screen → Tap microphone → Say “Open camera” → Voice confirmation → Camera/Add Product screen.
Do not only display the command as text.

VOICE-FIRST EXPERIENCE
Keep a floating microphone button visible on every major screen. When tapped, show:
- Listening state
- Detected language
- Transcribed speech
- Simple confirmation
- Suggested actions
- Cancel and confirm buttons

The assistant should feel like a friendly local-language helper, not a technical chatbot. Include voice responses such as:
“Opening the camera.”
“Your product details are ready.”
“Would you like to publish this product?”
“Your sales information is here.”

If the user says “Explain this page,” show a short explanation and optionally display the explanation as voice text. If the user says “Go back,” navigate to the previous screen.

HOME SCREEN
Create a simple home screen with:
1. Large “Take a Photo” button
2. Large “My Sales” button
3. Large “Find Materials” button
4. Small marketplace preview showing finished products
5. Government Help shortcut
6. Floating microphone
7. Profile/language control

The home screen should not look like a complex business dashboard. Prioritize three obvious actions and make the next step clear.

MAIN PRODUCT FLOW
Build this fully connected flow:
Language Selection → Home → Take Photo → AI Processing → Speak Product Details → Product Catalog → Fair Price → Publish → Success.

Show a simple progress indicator:
Photo → Tell → Price → Sell

TAKE PHOTO SCREEN
Show a large camera preview area with:
- Take Photo button
- Upload from Gallery button
- Retake button
- Continue button
- Voice instruction: “Take a clear photo of your product.”

After taking or uploading a photo, navigate to the AI Processing screen.

AI PROCESSING SCREEN
Show a realistic simulated AI process with short status updates:
- Recognizing product
- Removing background
- Improving lighting
- Preparing product image
- Creating catalog details

Show a before-and-after product image using a simulated handwoven cotton saree, handicraft, basket, or textile. The final image should look clean and suitable for online selling. Include a Continue button and a voice option.

VOICE PRODUCT DETAILS SCREEN
Allow the artisan to speak naturally in Malayalam or another selected language. Show:
- Detected language
- Voice transcription
- Translation preview
- Edit button
- Confirm button

Collect simple details such as:
- Product name
- Material used
- Craft type
- Quantity available
- Material cost
- Number of work days
- Other expenses

Use example Malayalam speech and show how it becomes structured product information. Keep the fields editable but do not require typing for the main flow.

CATALOG SCREEN
Display:
- Enhanced product image
- Short product title
- Simple professional description
- Material
- Craft type
- Quantity
- Seller information
- Language tabs for Malayalam, English, and Hindi
- Edit details button
- Continue to Fair Price button

The generated description should be short, attractive, and suitable for customers. Make it clear that the artisan can review or edit the AI-generated content.

FAIR PRICING SCREEN
Show a large suggested price, for example ₹2,600. Explain the price in simple language using a transparent breakdown:
- Material cost
- Labour value
- Other expenses
- Artisan profit
- Market reference

Include plus and minus controls or an editable price field. Show a simple message such as:
“This price includes your work and gives you a fair profit.”

Do not make pricing look like a complex financial tool. Include:
- Accept Price
- Edit Price
- Continue to Publish

PUBLISH SCREEN
Show a final product preview with:
- Product image
- Product title
- Price
- Quantity
- Selected languages
- Publish Product button

After publishing, navigate to a success screen with:
“Your product is now ready for customers.”
Show:
- View Product
- Share Product
- Go to My Sales
- Back to Home

MARKETPLACE PREVIEW
Create a simple marketplace area for finished products. Show product cards with:
- Image
- Product name
- Artisan-made label
- Price
- View button

Also create a separate “Find Materials” section for raw materials and byproducts. Include examples such as yarn, cotton, bamboo, fabric, dyes, tools, and packaging materials. This section should be searchable through voice and should not be confused with the finished-product marketplace.

MY SALES / MY BUSINESS SCREEN
Create a very simple business overview showing:
- Money received
- Products sold
- Pending orders
- Ongoing transactions
- Best-selling product
- Customer reviews
- Simple business insight

Use understandable statements such as:
“Your cotton saree is selling well.”
“You received ₹4,800 this month.”
“You have 2 orders waiting.”
Avoid complicated charts and technical analytics.

TRANSACTION TRUST SCREEN
Add a simple trust and verification screen. Explain the process visually:
Completed → Verified → Trust Points

Show verified transaction history and a trust score that increases after successful transactions. Do not lead with the word blockchain or expose technical blockchain details in the main interface. Explain it only in a small optional section as:
“Secure technology helps verify transactions and build trust.”

GOVERNMENT HELP SCREEN
Create a Government Help section with:
- Available schemes
- Financial support
- Training programs
- Tool and material assistance
- Eligibility information
- Application guidance
- Save or bookmark scheme

Use simple cards and a voice command such as:
“Show schemes for artisans.”
“Explain this scheme.”
“Am I eligible?”

GLOBAL NAVIGATION
Use a bottom navigation bar with:
Home, Products, My Sales, Government Help, Profile

Keep the microphone floating above the navigation bar. The microphone must remain accessible throughout the app and must support real navigation actions.

DEMO QUALITY
Use realistic simulated AI results, product images, voice transcription, translations, pricing values, transaction data, and success messages. Prioritize a complete three-minute hackathon demonstration:
1. Select Malayalam
2. Tap microphone
3. Say “Open camera”
4. Take or upload a product photo
5. AI enhances the image
6. Speak product details in Malayalam
7. Generate multilingual catalog
8. Show transparent fair price
9. Publish product
10. Open My Sales using voice
11. Show verified transaction and Government Help

Make the app feel like a helpful voice-powered assistant for artisans, not a normal shopping app. Ensure all transitions, buttons, language selection, microphone commands, and back navigation work correctly.
